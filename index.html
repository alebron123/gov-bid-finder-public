What this is

Every open US federal contract opportunity, plus State of Florida solicitations, in one searchable place. The federal data comes from the SAM.gov daily public extract; the Florida data comes from MyFloridaMarketPlace.

Why it exists

Governments buy ordinary things constantly: painting, landscaping, janitorial, IT support, catering, translation. Most small businesses never bid, because each notice is a wall of paperwork. Contracts under $100,000 routinely draw only a handful of bidders.

The full version

This public page is read-only. The full app adds Claude: describe your business in plain English and it builds a bidding profile, scores every open notice against it, explains any notice in plain English, and drafts a complete response package with cover letter, technical approach, pricing table, and submission checklist.

Source data is public. Always verify against the official notice before bidding.
